Maintainers review and merge pull requests, triage issues, keep the roadmap current and handle conduct and
security reports.

## How decisions are made

- **Everyday changes** (bug fixes, screens, docs, starter roadmap items) are merged after one maintainer
  approves and the tests pass.
- **Contract changes and anything security-sensitive** need a design note in the issue first and a
  maintainer's explicit approval before code is merged.
- **Roadmap changes** (new tracks, reprioritising, the mainnet gates) are proposed in an issue labelled
  `roadmap`, discussed in the open, and decided by the maintainers.

Where people disagree, the maintainers decide, and say why in the issue.

## Becoming a maintainer

Contributors who have landed several meaningful pull requests, reviewed others' work well, and shown good
judgement on the project's rules (especially data honesty and key safety) may be invited to become
maintainers of an area — for example Logistics, Port or the explorer. Ask in an issue if you are interested.
