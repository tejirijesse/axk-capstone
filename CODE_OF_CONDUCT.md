# Code of Conduct

This project serves cooperatives, traders and inspectors across East Africa, and its contributors come from
many places, languages and levels of experience. We want taking part to be welcoming for all of them.

This code is based on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers and people writing in their second or third language.
- Give and take feedback on the work, not the person. Explain the *why* behind a review comment.
- Assume good intent, and ask before concluding otherwise.
- Credit other people's work and ideas.
- Be honest about what the software does and does not do — including in issues, demos and pull requests.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing anyone's private information (addresses, phone numbers, keys) without their permission.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Repeatedly derailing discussions after being asked to stop.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions, chat — and when you represent the
project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report problems privately to the maintainers listed in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md). Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that break this code and may warn, suspend or ban
a contributor, in proportion to the harm caused.
